Helpful services

We're here for the hard stuff too.

Practical, confidential help for anyone going through a difficult time — no referral needed, no judgement, no strings attached.

Get in touch

HOW WE CAN HELP

Services available to everyone.

Food bank

Emergency food parcels for individuals and families in crisis. We work with the Colchester Foodbank — referral slips available at our reception or through local partners.

Debt advice

Free, confidential debt advice through our partnership with CAP (Christians Against Poverty). No appointment necessary to make a first enquiry.

Foodbank referrals

Not sure where to start? Come and speak to someone at the welcome desk — we can point you towards the right local services for your situation.

Community connections

We know Colchester. If you need a service, a group, or a person — we'll do our best to connect you. Drop in during office hours.

Pastoral care

Whether you're part of the church or not — if you're struggling, a member of our pastoral team is available to listen and pray with you.

Bereavement support

One-to-one or group support for those who have lost someone. Ask at reception for more information.

Not sure if we can help? Just ask.

We'd rather you reach out and find out than stay stuck. Everything is confidential, and there's no obligation.
