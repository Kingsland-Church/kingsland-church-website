Generosity

Thank you for giving.

Everything Kingsland does — our services, community programmes, and support for people in need — is made possible by generous people who believe in this place.

Give online or in person.

Give in person

Collection bags are passed around during Sunday services. Cash, cheques payable to Kingsland Christian Fellowship, or contactless via our card reader at the exit.

Gift Aid

If you're a UK taxpayer, Gift Aid means we receive an extra 25p for every £1 you give — at no extra cost to you. Ask at the welcome desk or email us to complete a declaration.

WHERE IT GOES

Your giving in action.

We're committed to being good stewards of everything entrusted to us.

Sunday services

Keeping the building open, the lights on, and the worship team supported every week.

Kids & youth work

Safe, joyful groups for young people — from crèche to teens — every Sunday.

Community support

Food bank, helpful services, and care for people facing hardship across Colchester.

Alpha

Hosting and feeding dozens of people exploring faith every term.

The Community Centre

Subsidised space for local charities, community groups, and families who need it.

Global mission

Supporting partner churches and mission projects around the world.
