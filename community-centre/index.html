Community Centre

Open to the whole community.

A welcoming hub in the heart of Colchester — with a café, affordable room hire, and services that help people thrive.

Everything under one roof.

Freshly made food, great coffee, and a warm welcome — open through the week. A place to meet, work, or just slow down.
