SUNDAYS

Four services to choose from.

Whether Sunday morning, Sunday evening, or a midweek afternoon — there's a service that fits your week.

🕘

Sunday 9:15am

A quieter, family-friendly start — great if you have young children or prefer a calmer pace.

🕚

Sunday 11:00am

Our largest service — buzzing, welcoming, and a great place to meet new people over coffee.

🌙

Sunday 6:30pm

An intimate evening gathering in The Café — smaller, more relaxed. Perfect if mornings don't work for you.

☀

Wednesday@1 · 1pm

A warm midweek service at 1pm — smaller, unhurried, and welcoming to all. Especially popular with those who find Sundays harder to make.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Honest answers to the questions you're probably thinking.

How long is it?

About 75–90 minutes. There's sung worship, a talk, and time to chat over coffee afterwards.

What should I wear?

Anything you like. Seriously. People come in suits, jeans, and everything in between.

Will I be put on the spot?

Never. You can be completely invisible if you want to be — or jump straight in. Up to you.

What about my kids?

We have brilliant groups for every age from 0 upwards, running alongside both services.

Is it just for Christians?

Not at all. Come curious, come sceptical, come with questions — you'll fit right in.

Do I need to book?

No booking needed. Just turn up — there's always a seat and always a welcome team at the door.
