Room hire

A space for your community.

Bright, affordable rooms in the centre of Colchester — loved by local charities, businesses, and families. From an intimate meeting room to a 200-seat hall.

Book a room Enquire now

OUR SPACES

Choose your room.

Charter Hall

Our flagship space — 200 theatre-style seats, a raised stage, full AV system, and a separate green room. Perfect for conferences, performances, and large events.

  • ✓  Capacity: up to 200 (theatre) / 120 (banquet)
  • ✓  Stage, full AV, hearing loop
  • ✓  Adjacent kitchen and catering prep
  • ✓  Fully accessible

Garden Room

A light-filled room overlooking the garden — ideal for smaller meetings, workshops, and training days. Up to 40 people.

  • ✓  Capacity: up to 40
  • ✓  Projector and whiteboard
  • ✓  Access to kitchen for teas/coffees
  • ✓  Ground floor, fully accessible

Meeting rooms

Two smaller rooms suitable for board meetings, supervision, and small groups. Both on the ground floor with natural light.

  • ✓  Capacity: 8–12 per room
  • ✓  Screen / TV available
  • ✓  Wi-Fi throughout

The Café

Available for private hire outside café hours — great for small parties, networking evenings, or informal gatherings. Up to 60 people.

  • ✓  Capacity: up to 60
  • ✓  Catering from our café team available
  • ✓  Central, welcoming atmosphere
