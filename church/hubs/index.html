Kids & Youth

A brilliant place to grow up.

Safe, joyful, and age-appropriate groups for every child — from crèche through to teens — running during both Sunday services.

Plan your first visit

SUNDAY GROUPS

Every age is welcome.

All groups run during the 9:15am and 11:00am Sunday services. Children begin in the service and are dismissed to their groups after worship. The 6:30pm and Wednesday@1 services do not have separate children's groups.

Crèche & Toddlers

0–3 years

A safe, nurturing environment for babies and toddlers. Parents are welcome to stay — or drop off and enjoy the service.

Kingsland Kids

4–7 years

Games, stories, and crafts based on the Bible. A lively, fun environment where faith comes alive.

Primary

8–11 years

Discussions, creative activities, and big questions. Engaging content for kids who are ready to go deeper.

Youth

12–18 years

A space for teenagers to belong, ask questions, and encounter God. Meets across the week too — ask us for details.

SAFEGUARDING

Safety is our top priority.

All Kingsland children's and youth workers are DBS-checked and trained in safeguarding. We follow the Church of England's safeguarding guidelines and have a dedicated safeguarding lead.

Contact our safeguarding team
