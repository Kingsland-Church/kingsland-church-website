Hubs & Groups

Communities within the community.

Kingsland's hubs are distinct communities — each with its own focus, lead and rhythm — working together as one church. There's a place for everyone.

Get in touch

WHAT IS A HUB?

Not a programme — a community.

Each hub is a community of people who share a common passion or ministry. Hubs have their own leads and boards of reference, and they gather, serve and grow together within the wider Kingsland family.

Hubs are open to everyone — you don't need to be a church member, and there's no commitment required to show up for the first time.

8 hubs — each with its own lead and focus
40+ groups and activities — run almost entirely by volunteers
All ages welcome — from newborns to older adults

YOUTH HUB — REMIX

For young people in years 7–11.

Remix is Kingsland's youth community — helping young people see, know and follow Jesus. Led by Shaun Almond, who moved from the US in 2016 to lead Kingsland's youth work.

  • Sunday mornings — during both services
  • Tuesday evenings — midweek hangout
  • Friday nights — social and fun

CHILDREN & FAMILY HUB

Families and children of all ages.

Led by Bec Bright, the Children & Family Hub runs groups for babies through to pre-teens every Sunday, as well as Messy Church, toddler groups and other family activities through the week.

  • Crèche (0–3) — Sunday mornings
  • Kingsland Kids (4–7) — Sunday mornings
  • Primary (8–11) — Sunday mornings
  • Toddler groups and Messy Church midweek
Find out more →

HERE FOR GOOD HUB

Compassion, care and community action.

Led by Samuel Okafor, Here For Good is Kingsland's charitable and compassion arm — serving the most vulnerable in Colchester through practical, life-changing support.

  • Precious Bundles — baby essentials for new parents
  • Free school holiday lunches for local children
  • Kingsland Counselling — professional counselling services
  • Other outreach and support initiatives

PRAYER & SPIRITUALITY HUB

Seeking God together.

Led by Gail Mitchell, this hub nurtures Kingsland's diverse prayer life and helps people grow in their spiritual journey — through daily prayer, church prayer gatherings and formation groups.

  • Daily prayer meetings
  • Church-wide prayer evenings
  • Soaking and contemplative prayer sessions
  • Spiritual formation and discipleship
Find out more →

HEALTH & WELLBEING HUB

Whole-person health for everyone.

Led by Kingsley and Sharon Osei-Tutu — both local GPs — this hub promotes health and wellbeing across the church and community through activities, education and connection.

  • Health seminars and talks
  • Physical activity groups
  • Kingsland Choir
  • Mental health support signposting

PASTORAL HUB

Care for every season of life.

Led by Ian Davies, the Pastoral Hub maintains an environment where people can grow — celebrating success, walking through hard times, and building a genuinely caring church community.

  • Pastoral care and support
  • Discipleship groups

  • Wednesday@1 congregation

OUTREACH HUB

Reaching beyond our walls.

Led by Karen Loring, the Outreach Hub coordinates the ways Kingsland actively reaches out to the city — through Alpha, Healing Rooms, and other evangelism and community engagement initiatives.

  • Alpha — exploring faith in a relaxed setting
  • Healing Rooms — prayer for healing

CAFÉ & CENTRE HUB

The heartbeat of the building.

Led by Ade and Maggie, this hub runs the Kingsland Café and the day-to-day life of the Community Centre — creating a welcoming space for church, community and neighbours alike, Monday to Saturday.

  • Kingsland Café — open Mon–Sat
  • Room hire and facility management
  • Community groups and hirers

SAFEGUARDING

Safety is our top priority.

All Kingsland workers with children and vulnerable adults are DBS-checked and trained in safeguarding. We have a dedicated safeguarding lead and follow recognised best-practice guidelines.
