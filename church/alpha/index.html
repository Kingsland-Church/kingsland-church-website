Alpha

What's the meaning of life?

Alpha is a series of relaxed conversations about life's big questions — over a meal, with no pressure and no expectation. Anyone is welcome. Seriously.

Find out when next Alpha starts

WHAT IS ALPHA?

A relaxed space to explore.

Each session starts with food, then there's a short talk on a different question about life and faith, followed by a small-group discussion where you can say anything — or nothing at all.

🍕

It starts with food

Every Alpha session begins with a meal together. It's a chance to relax and get to know people before the discussion starts.

💬

Real conversations

A short video or talk on a topic — Is there a God? Why does suffering exist? What happens when we die? Then an open discussion.

✌️

No pressure at all

Nothing is expected. You don't have to say anything, believe anything, or come back next week. Just come curious.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you commit.

Who is Alpha for?

Anyone — Christians, sceptics, curious people, atheists, and those who just want good food and an interesting conversation.

How long does it run?

Alpha typically runs for 10 weekly sessions, plus a weekend away (optional). Each evening is about 2 hours including food.

Do I need to book?

Yes — just so we know how much food to make! Get in touch to register your interest and we'll confirm dates when the next course starts.

Is it free?

Yes. Alpha is completely free — including the food. Just bring yourself.

Ready to give it a try?

Get in touch and we'll let you know when the next Alpha starts. No commitment required — just let us know you're interested.
