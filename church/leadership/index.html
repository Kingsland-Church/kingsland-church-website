Leadership

People who serve so others can flourish.

Kingsland is led by a team of senior leaders, hub leads, trustees and hundreds of volunteers — all working together to love God and our community.

BOARDS OF REFERENCE

Ideas, insight and direction.

The Boards of Reference work with hub leads to bring ideas, insight and direction to the church. They represent a diverse group of ages and backgrounds with significant influence over the direction and activities of Kingsland.

TRUSTEES

Legal & financial oversight.

Our Trustees are responsible for all the legal and financial aspects of the church, ensuring Kingsland operates with integrity and accountability as a registered charity.

VOLUNTEERS

The people who make it all happen.

Kingsland completely depends on a huge army of volunteers — from serving in the café and youth work to organising, facilitating and serving in all sorts of capacities. There are around 40 groups and activities, and every one of them runs because people give their time.
