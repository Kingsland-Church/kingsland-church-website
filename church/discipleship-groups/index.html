Community groups

Smaller gatherings, deeper friendships.

Midweek groups meeting across Colchester — for friendship, prayer, Bible study, and doing life together. There's a group for you.

Find a group near you

WHAT HAPPENS

What a community group looks like.

👋

Gather

Groups of 8–14 people, meeting in homes across Colchester. Usually fortnightly on a weekday evening.

📖

Study

An accessible look at a passage of Scripture or a topic — with plenty of space for questions and discussion.

🙏

Pray

Groups pray together for each other and for the church — in whatever way feels natural for where you are.

JOIN A GROUP

We'll find the right fit.

Groups are spread across Colchester — from Lexden to Greenstead — with a mix of ages and life stages. We'll help you find the one that fits where you live and what your week looks like.

New groups also form each term, so if you can't find the right one now, there may be one starting soon.
