Our story

Over 140 years in Colchester.

From a Quaker temperance hall in 1884 to a thriving community church and centre — Kingsland has always been about people.

Plan your visit
1884

Founded in Lexden

Local Quakers built a hall in Lexden as a place of worship, temperance and education — running an adult school on Sundays and a people's bank for the community.

1971

Lexden Evangelical Church

Following the appointment of a new leader, the church was established as Lexden Evangelical Church and began to take on a more evangelical identity.

1980s

Kingsland is born

The church became much more vibrant, adopting a lively and contemporary worship style. We changed our name to Kingsland Church and started two other congregations nearby.

2005

A new building

After adding a second Sunday morning service and continuing to grow, we built a new building on the same Lexden site to meet the needs of our expanding congregation.

2010

86 London Road

We moved to much larger premises on the top floor of the former MFI building on the corner of London Road and Straight Road. Our facilities now include a 400-seat worship area, large hall, nine meeting rooms, offices, and a large café — open to church and community alike.

2020

Owning our home

We purchased the virtual freehold of the whole site. Aldi moved out in 2021, opening up significant possibilities for the future of the building and our community work.

Today

A church and community for everyone

Kingsland is an independent church with strong links to other Colchester fellowships. We welcome input from various church streams and continue to grow our staff, leadership and community profile — loving our city and one another.
